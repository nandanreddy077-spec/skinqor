Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

Skinqor AI (“we”, “our”, or “Skinqor”) provides an AI-powered skin analysis widget that skincare and beauty brands embed on their websites. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our website, join our waitlist, or when end customers use the Skinqor AI widget on a merchant’s site.

1. Information We Collect

On this website (skinqorai.com)

  • Waitlist and contact: When you sign up for early access or contact us, we collect your email address and, if you choose to provide it, your name and brand/business name.
  • Usage data: We may collect general usage data (e.g. pages visited, referral source) to improve our site and services.

When the Skinqor AI widget is used on a merchant’s site

  • Skin analysis: To provide personalized product recommendations, our technology may process images (e.g. selfies) and derive skin-related insights (e.g. skin type, concerns such as dehydration or sun damage). This processing is done to match the end user to products sold by the merchant whose site they are on.
  • Technical and usage data: We may collect device and browser information, session data, and interaction data related to the widget to operate and improve our service.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their marketing.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Skinqor AI widget and our website.
  • To communicate with you about early access, product updates, and support.
  • To analyze usage and improve our algorithms and user experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

3. Data Storage and Security

We store data using industry-standard practices and service providers. We take reasonable steps to protect personal and skin-analysis data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Data may be stored in jurisdictions where our service providers operate.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share information with:

  • Merchants: When you use the widget on a merchant’s site, that merchant may receive skin insights and product recommendations relevant to their store, in line with our agreement with them.
  • Service providers: We use third-party services for hosting, analytics, and operations; they process data on our instructions and under appropriate agreements.
  • Legal: We may disclose information where required by law or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

5. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights or ask questions, contact us at anixagency7@gmail.com. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time via the link in our emails.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and widget may use cookies and similar technologies for essential operation, analytics, and improving your experience. You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings.

7. Children

Our service is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe we have collected such data, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and, where appropriate, notify you by email or through our service. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the latest change.

9. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at anixagency7@gmail.com.
